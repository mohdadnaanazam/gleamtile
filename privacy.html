Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Gleamtile ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. We built it to work without collecting your personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your level progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, photos, contacts, location, microphone or any other protected data. If you choose to share an artwork, the system share sheet handles it.

Children

Because we collect no personal data, the app is safe for players of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date.
