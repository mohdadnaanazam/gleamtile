Subscriptions

Gleamtile Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription ($1.99/month or $19.99/year in the US). You can cancel any time in your device's Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open Gleamtile → Settings → Restore Purchases.
